Platform billing user guide

Software fees for a website are separate from ticket sales, memberships, school fees, and marketplace settlements. This page says who turns a charge on and what each person sees.

A platform super-admin opens /admin/platform-billing. A tenant admin of the website that pays opens /admin/billing.

1. Who can turn a charge on

Only a platform super-admin can turn the charge switch on, set a custom fee, or change grace days. While the switch is off, the website is complimentary and Stripe is not called.

Turning the switch on sets the status to payment due and starts the grace window (today plus the grace days). The payer still has to open Pay. The switch does not charge the card by itself.

2. Organization or each website

On the organization page, Billing scope has two choices.

ChoiceWhat happens
Bill the organization onceOne monthly amount. One retail subscription on the organization’s home website, noted organization-flat. Every other website is covered, its switch is off, and a website subscription is canceled at period end.
Bill each website separatelyThe organization amount is cleared and the organization subscription is canceled at period end. Covered notes are removed. Each website stays complimentary until a super-admin turns that website’s switch on. Nothing is auto-charged.
The Stripe customer stays on the organization in both modes, so the card belongs to the client.
3. What the paying admin sees
  • If the website is covered, the page names the organization that pays. Pay and Cancel are hidden.
  • If this website pays, the page shows the plan, any custom amount, status, grace, and the local charge list.
  • Pay opens Stripe Checkout. Payment methods opens the Stripe Customer Portal. There is no card form inside this app.
Money is shown with two decimal places, for example $1.00.
4. Cancel and lock
ActionWhoResult
Cancel at period endThe tenant admin who pays for that website, or a super-adminAccess continues until the period ends. Stripe then sends subscription deleted and the ledger records the close.
Cancel nowSuper-admin onlyThe subscription stops now and the row returns to complimentary.
LockAutomaticWhen grace has ended, or Stripe reports a failed payment, paid admin tools for that tenant redirect to the billing page. The public site stays up. A complimentary or covered website is not locked for another website’s unpaid invoice.
5. Moving a website

Moving a website into an organization that is billed once covers that website and turns its own charge switch off. Moving it to an organization that bills each website separately removes the covered note. The website does not start paying until a super-admin turns its switch on.

Do not use organizations 18, 19, or 20 for a billing experiment. Create a new billing-test organization.
